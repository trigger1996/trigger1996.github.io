Brief introduction:

`Enforcement of Opacity for Interval Weighted Automata` implements supervisory-control algorithms for enforcing opacity in interval weighted automata and interval weighted discrete-event systems. The software accompanies research on opacity enforcement under interval-valued weights and supervisory control.
