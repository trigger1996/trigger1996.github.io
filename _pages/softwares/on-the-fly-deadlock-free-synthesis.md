Brief introduction:

`On-the-Fly Deadlock-Free Synthesis` provides algorithms and case studies for synthesizing deadlock-free supervisors for discrete-event systems. The on-the-fly implementation avoids constructing the full state space upfront and is designed for supervisor-synthesis workflows where deadlock avoidance is the main correctness requirement.
