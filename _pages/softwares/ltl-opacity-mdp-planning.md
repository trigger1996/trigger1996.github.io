Brief introduction:

`LTL-Based Opacity Planning for MDPs` supports planning for Markov decision processes under temporal-logic specifications and LTL-based opacity constraints. The opacity requirement asks the synthesized behavior to remain indistinguishable with respect to the satisfied optimizing atomic propositions, so that the system's true intention is hidden from an intruder.
