Brief introduction:

`Detectable Nonblocking Supervisory Control` provides algorithms and case studies for enforcing detectable nonblocking in partially observed discrete-event systems. Detectable nonblocking is a stronger requirement than standard nonblocking in the partial-observation setting, where the supervisor must ensure nonblocking behavior based on information available from observations.
