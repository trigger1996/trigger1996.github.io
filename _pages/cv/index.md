Yiwei Zheng
======

* Postdoctoral Researcher
* School of Automation and Intelligent Sensing, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, China
* Date of birth: Dec. 1, 1996
* Email: [ywzheng1201@sjtu.edu.cn](mailto:ywzheng1201@sjtu.edu.cn)

Research Interest
======

* Multi-agent path and mission planning using formal methods.
* Safety, security, and opacity of discrete-event systems.
* Control and navigation integration for robotic systems in unknown environments.
