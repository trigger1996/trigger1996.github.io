Yiwei Zheng is a postdoctoral researcher at the School of Automation and Intelligent Sensing, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, hosted by Prof. Xiang Yin.

He received his Ph.D. degree in Control Science and Engineering from Xiamen University, China, under the supervision of Prof. Weiyao Lan and Prof. Xiao Yu.

His research focuses on discrete-event systems and formal methods, with particular interest in robotic planning and information-state-based properties of discrete dynamic systems, including safety, security, and opacity. His work also covers multi-agent path and mission planning and control-navigation integration for robotic systems in unknown environments.

Education
======

* 2018-2025: Ph.D., Control Science and Engineering, Xiamen University, Xiamen, China.
* 2014-2018: B.S., Opto-Electronic Science and Engineering, Huaqiao University, Quanzhou, China.

Research Interests
======

* Multi-agent path and mission planning using formal methods.
* Safety, security, and opacity of discrete-event systems.
* Control and navigation integration for robotic systems in unknown environments.
