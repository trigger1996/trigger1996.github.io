Yiwei Zheng is a Ph.D. candidate in Control Science and Engineering at Xiamen University, China, advised by Prof. Weiyao Lan and Prof. Xiao Yu.

His research focuses on formal methods for robotic and discrete-event systems, including multi-agent path and mission planning, safety and security, opacity, and control-navigation integration for robotic systems in unknown environments.

Education
======

* 2018-present: Ph.D., Control Science and Engineering, Xiamen University, Xiamen, China.
* 2014-2018: B.S., Opto-Electronic Science and Engineering, Huaqiao University, Quanzhou, China.

Research Interests
======

* Multi-agent path and mission planning using formal methods.
* Safety, security, and opacity of discrete-event systems.
* Control and navigation integration for robotic systems in unknown environments.
