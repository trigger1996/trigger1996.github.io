* I was born in [Wuyishan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuyishan,_Fujian), Fujian, China.

* I received the B.S. degree in Opto-Electronic Science and Engineering from [Huaqiao University](https://www.hqu.edu.cn/), Quanzhou, China, 2018.

* I received the Ph.D. degree in Control Science and Engineering from [Xiamen University](https://www.xmu.edu.cn/), Xiamen, China, 2025, supervised by Prof. Weiyao Lan and Prof. Xiao Yu.

* I joined the [School of Automation and Intelligent Sensing](https://sais.sjtu.edu.cn/), Shanghai Jiao Tong University, as a postdoctoral researcher in January, 2026, hosted by Prof. [Xiang Yin](https://xiangyin.sjtu.edu.cn/).
